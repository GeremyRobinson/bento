# Obento² Swift app: how we work here

You're working with G, who owns Obento², a maths app for kids (K-12) that shows a child why the maths works, then practises it with them every day. Talk in plain words; G isn't a programmer. Always write the name as "Obento²", never "Bento 2".

## The app
- `Obento2.swiftpm` is the app. G runs it in Swift Playgrounds on the Mac (no Xcode). After a change, tell G to press Play. If Playgrounds doesn't refresh, close and reopen the project.
- `App/` holds the screens. The whole paper is one RealityKit scene seen through one camera (`PaperScene.swift`): grid, lesson pictures and 3D lessons alike; flat is that camera straight above. Words and controls are SwiftUI on top, pinned to the paper. `Core/` holds the lesson logic and camera maths, with no drawing in it.
- Xcode is installed (`/Applications/Xcode.app`) but not selected, so prefix commands with `DEVELOPER_DIR=/Applications/Xcode.app/Contents/Developer`. Build and screenshot locally on the iPad simulator (`xcodegen`, then `xcodebuild` and `simctl launch … -shot <name>`) before pushing; it's much faster than waiting for GitHub.
- Test Core with `cd Packages/ObentoCore && swift test` (it links to `Obento2.swiftpm/Core`). Every lesson's maths gets tests.
- The look comes from the web prototype (the "paper canvas", v27): `reference/paper-canvas.html`. Open it in a browser and match it or beat it. The Unit cubes 3D lesson is what sold the idea.
- How every lesson is taught, drawn and animated: `LESSONS.md`. Read it before building or changing any lesson, and follow it.
- Branch `claude/project-thread-15w6ys`. `git pull` before each change and push after it; other sessions push here too. A push makes GitHub build it and save iPad screenshots in `shots/`.

- **All the lessons:** `specs/k12/` (191 K-12 lessons) and `specs/obento2/` (154 Obento² lessons) are the Curriculum specs. The live web app's lesson code (generators, answer checks) is in `web/src/curriculum/` on `main`: read it, port its maths with the same answers, and never change the web app.

## G's rules (firm)
- **The paper is the app.** Everything is drawn on the grid and uses its cells: 20 pt cells, a strong line every 5. One cube = one big square. Nothing goes off-grid, and the grid never changes or fades when a lesson opens. It's graph paper (G 17 Oct): frames and boxes start and end on the strong lines and are spaced by whole blocks (map frames 3 × 2 blocks with one block below for the name; Start is the frame's tag, replacing Up next when chosen; problem boxes 6 × 9).
- **No iOS look.** No big bright or dark buttons, no side panel, no pop-ups. Controls are cell blocks drawn on the paper. Colour lives only in the maths; red and green mean only wrong and right.
- **Never remove an animation** (G, 17 Oct): the draw-ins, the glide, the cubes rising, the bars filling, the point hopping, and every web lesson's animated picture when it's ported. A ported lesson isn't done until its picture moves step by step as the web's does.
- **Motion has a purpose.** Pieces draw themselves in. Tapping a lesson is a gentle glide (0.75 s) to 100% (G 17 Oct, with the bigger 3 × 2 block frames; was 125%). Each step of a lesson frames what it shows now, zoomed to fit and centred, up to 125% (`CameraTiming.selectMax`, `lessonMax`). No looping animations. Never remove drawing or zoom; G counts that as a step back.
- **3D lessons** open flat, lined up with the paper exactly. They tilt only when the maths rises off the paper, words stay pinned to the paper facing the child, and the view goes flat again before leaving.
- **Every problem is fresh** (from a generator), and the answer is worked out from the same numbers as the picture.
- **Icons are Obento²'s own** (G 17 Oct): drawn like the lesson pictures, thin square-ended lines on a 24-unit grid with one shape softly tinted (`ObentoIcons.swift`); squares and gaps measured like the Lessons icon. Phosphor stays only where an icon isn't drawn yet. Font: SF Pro Rounded for now (`Typeface` in `Theme.swift`).
- **One floating bar at the bottom** (G 17 Oct, `Toolbar.swift`): a pill fixed to the screen. Map: Lessons · Me | grade and progress | ⋯. Lesson: ← Lessons | lesson and progress | Hint · Show me | ⋯. Everything else lives in ⋯ More. A pinch shows the zoom in the bar's middle at 22 pt (the size G likes). G tried a dial instead and found it too complicated; Today will move into Me.
- **Plain words** (G 18 Oct): every line tells the child what to do, short and clear ("Shade eighths to match 3/4.", "Continue", "Practice"). No teacher phrasing, no extra labels like "Why it works", no questions they didn't ask.
- **G approves first.** Show G (a screenshot, or "press Play") before calling anything done. Change only what G asked for. Every change must be better than what came before.

## What's next

- Milestone 3: every lesson is data (`Core/LessonEngine.swift`). A `LessonSpec` generates fresh numbers and works out its steps from them: words, the marking task with its checks and mistake messages, the camera, and what the picture shows. `LessonRun` plays any spec; `Lessons.all` is the catalog the map opens from, and the spec's `master` ("cubes", "bars") picks the picture master that draws it. Adding a lesson = a spec in Core with tests, a map piece, and a master if no existing one fits.
- **Read `LESSONS.md` before building or changing any lesson.** It is G's rulebook for how lessons are taught and animated on the paper (four beats Ask/Do/See/Say, hand moves, animation timings, camera frames, mistakes drawn, hints, Show me, practice, skill check). A lesson is built in Swift only after G's OK on its web preview (§9). On 17 Oct G chose: build the engine pieces LESSONS.md needs on the two approved lessons (Unit cubes, Add with different bottoms), and keep the other 15 lessons locked on the map (`Map.paperLessons`) until their previews are approved. Their maths, words and tests are already ported in Core.
- G's corrections to LESSONS.md (17 Oct): ignore its "text stays 20 pt and lines 3 pt at any zoom" (a web-thinking mistake): words and lines scale with the paper. **Show me is a preview, never the child's work:** it plays the step on the paper so the child can watch, then puts everything back as the child left it. New hand moves (drag, cut, group, place a point) come with the lessons whose previews use them.
- **On the paper, the problems live on the paper** (G, 17 Oct): the web app shows a list of problems behind a button; Obento² puts every problem on the paper itself. To be designed after milestone 5.
- Progress (milestone 5, Core/Mastery.swift, the web's rules): learned = last skill check 3 or 4 and not due back; rechecks after 3, 7, 21, 60 days (a miss: tomorrow); Me's shelves Go over / Getting stronger / Stuck; Today's pick. A due lesson opens on its recheck. Their own screens (Me, the grown-up page, Today) wait for previews.

- **The lesson plan G agreed on 18 Oct 2026** (build in this order):
  1. **Saving and resume.** Every problem's work is saved as it happens (shading, solved, which problem). Back the same day: no How to, open on the problem you were on. Back after a few days: a short "Remember?" How to you can tap to skip. "Watch the How to again" in ⋯. The map shows "Not finished · 5 of 16".
  2. **The How to, child-paced and why first.** One tap per step instead of a timer; it opens with the lesson's idea drawn on the paper.
  3. **A "we do" problem.** Problems 1–2 are solved together: the paper points to each move and the child makes it.
  4. **Help fades across the page.** Rows 1–4 every step named, hints free; 5–8 pick the next step, fewer hints; 9–12 final answer only, steps come back after a miss; 13–16 the skill check. Clean early rows can offer the check early.
  5. **The web's learning rules.** Wrong answers sorted into types (sign, off by one, place value, digits, close, reused) each with its own line; step points (1, ½, ¼, 0) make the 0–4 score; hints limited; help also fades by last score.
  6. **Tests on the map.** Skill check in every lesson (rechecks 3, 7, 21, 60 days, a miss tomorrow); a chapter test (12 problems) appears at the end of a chapter's row once its lessons are learned, dashed until then; a grade test (20) once every chapter test is done; scores 0–4. Lessons stay open; only tests unlock.

  7. **Today inside Me**, with the daily review (8 problems weighted to weak and old lessons).
