## Decided by Claude (small calls; G 1 Nov: only direction comes back to G)
- **Each topic once, where the standard puts it.** The median is taught in 6th (g9-median stays as a short review),
  zero and negative exponents and distance between points in 8th (the web's g9-negexp and g10-dist move there,
  keeping their ids), Algebra 1 topics the web had in 11th stay in 11th, trig's start, the geometric sum, Pascal's
  triangle and log rules in 12th (not taught twice), chance in 10th.
- **Build order:** each chapter whole, in teaching order (a chapter opens complete, with its test).
- **The plane:** one block is one unit wherever the numbers fit (5th grade's Points on a grid); one square a unit
  only where the graph needs the room (11th's flips over y = x).
- **3D only where the maths rises off the paper:** K's positions, 1st's solids, 3rd's liter jug, and the volume and
  solid lessons in 6th–10th.
- **Kept as planned:** proofs as records with a reason picked from three blocks; Common Core's (+) extras on the map
  like any lesson; 12th's Matrices chapter; calculus stops at the derivative from secants, max/min and area by strips.
- **Pictures that would get small:** problems are capped so a picture is never framed below about 80%.
- **New hand moves** in each plan get a preview on the test page before they're built, as 5th grade's did.
