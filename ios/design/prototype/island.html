The pill as an island

The paper keeps the maths and the progress pictures. Everything you read or set (settings, privacy, help, and later the plan) grows out of the pill into a larger island, the way ⋯ More does, then shrinks back. Tap through the moments.

Why this split

  • Paper = maths and progress. Lessons, Me, and the grown-ups' This week and Lessons stay drawn on the grid. They're pictures of learning.
  • Island = the app itself. Settings, privacy, help and payments aren't maths, so they don't pretend to be on graph paper. They live in the frame that's always there.
  • One place for serious things. Anything a parent must read or agree to (privacy, a purchase) appears in the same calm island, behind the same hold.
  • Still ours, not iOS. Same surface, curve and icons as the pill; it grows out of it like a drawer and shrinks back into it. No sheet sliding over the screen, no system look.
